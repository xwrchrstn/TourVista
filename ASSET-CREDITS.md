These local stock images illustrate **fictional demo listings only**. They do not represent actual Claveria properties, their entrances or current conditions. Cards label them “Sample photo”; the gallery includes an explicit stock-photo disclosure. Real listings display the owner's supplied HTTPS image, or an original illustration when no photo is provided. No ownership evidence is included in this public asset folder.
